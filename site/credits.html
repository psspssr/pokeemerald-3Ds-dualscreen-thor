What the images show

The Thor hero is an AI-edited presentation mockup, using real emulator captures for its screens. It is not a photograph of this game running on a physical Thor and is not hardware-test evidence.

Device reference: DROIX-hosted AYN Thor product image. Complete mockup provenance.

Gallery images are unmodified screenshots of the actual ARM Android game in an emulator. Classic Oldale Town and Route 101 captures use the signed alpha.3 build; the earlier voxel, battle and phone images have their own recorded build identities. Normal gameplay inputs staged these gallery scenes, without save/progression or pixel edits.

The gallery uses a combined landscape window; the portrait image shows the phone layout. Physical Thor testing remains open. Capture records and checksums · Validation report.

The emerald-dragon app icon is generated artwork. Icon provenance.
