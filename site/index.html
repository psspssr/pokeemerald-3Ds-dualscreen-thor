BEFORE YOU SET OFF

Your Thor.
Your next adventure.

  1. 01

    Check your Thor’s firmware

    Android 9 or newer, OpenGL ES 3.0, and 32-bit ARM app support. Firmware that only runs 64-bit apps cannot run this preview.

  2. 02

    Bring your save

    Import an Emerald .sav in Settings, then restart. Export it to return to a GBA emulator. Save states are a different format.

  3. 03

    Make both screens yours

    Both Thor panels fill by default without cropping. Keep the adventure above and touch menus below, or choose Fit for original proportions.

Also supports compatible Android phones and other handhelds. Builds are tested in emulators; Thor hardware and sustained performance validation remain ongoing. See the test report ↗
